# Question 4: NotebookLM Experiment Log

**Date:** 11 October 2026  
**Scenario:** University student preparing an ESG sustainability research briefing  
**Platform:** Google NotebookLM

## Source Documents

1. GRI 303: Water and Effluents 2018
2. GRI 306: Waste 2020

## Experiment 1: Document Comparison and Citations

**Task:** Compare the purpose and disclosure requirements of GRI 303 and GRI 306.

**Observation:** NotebookLM generated a detailed explanation and comparison table with numbered source citations.

**Verification:** A citation relating to Disclosure 303-3 opened the relevant water withdrawal section in the GRI 303 source. This confirmed that the citation navigated to a relevant section, although not every numerical or technical statement was independently checked.

**Usefulness:** Helps students compare lengthy academic documents and identify relevant evidence.

**Limitation:** Citations require manual checking because a relevant source passage does not guarantee that every claim is accurate.

## Experiment 2: Interactive Learning Overview

**Task:** Generate university revision material from the two standards.

**Observation:** NotebookLM produced six structured sections covering water stewardship, water measurements, waste management and a summary.

**Usefulness:** Provides organised learning objectives and simplifies complex source material.

**Limitation:** Simplified explanations may omit detailed disclosure requirements and exceptions.

## Experiment 3: Sustainability Quiz

**Task:** Generate medium-difficulty revision questions using both sources.

**Observation:** NotebookLM generated a 12-question quiz. One tested question asked for the freshwater Total Dissolved Solids threshold under GRI 303. The selected answer of 1,000 mg/L or below was marked correct, with an explanation.

**Usefulness:** Supports self-assessment and immediate feedback.

**Limitation:** Only one question was examined in detail. The accuracy of all 12 questions has not been independently verified.

## Experiment 4: Mind Map

**Task:** Compare the disclosure structure of GRI 303 and GRI 306 visually.

**Observation:** The expanded map correctly displayed Water Withdrawal (303-3), Water Discharge (303-4), Water Consumption (303-5), Waste Generated (306-3), Waste Diverted from Disposal (306-4) and Waste Directed to Disposal (306-5).

**Usefulness:** Supports visual learning and helps students connect related reporting concepts.

**Limitation:** The mind map shows an overview rather than the full reporting requirements.

## Experiment 5: Audio Overview

**Task:** Create an audio discussion explaining the two sustainability standards.

**Observation:** NotebookLM generated a 5-minute, 29-second Audio Overview, and the playback interface was tested.

**Usefulness:** Provides an alternative format for students who prefer listening while revising.

**Limitation:** Detailed factual accuracy of the full recording has not been independently established. Precise technical requirements should be checked against the written standards.

## Overall Evaluation

NotebookLM demonstrated useful capabilities for source-based research, structured revision, quizzes, visual learning and audio learning. It is suitable for supplementary academic study, but should not replace original documents or independent verification of technical claims.

The evaluation was limited to two source documents and a small set of tests. A wider pilot would be needed before recommending university-wide adoption.
